Whale Sharks can be seen year-round in Saleh Bay because they inhabit the calm bay waters and feed near the traditional fisherman platforms (bagan). However, the best ocean conditions with optimal underwater visibility are generally from April to November.

We provide all high-quality snorkeling gear, life vests, and scuba equipment. We recommend bringing your swimsuit/rashguard, reef-safe sunscreen, a dry change of clothes, sunglasses, a towel, and personal medications if you are prone to seasickness.
